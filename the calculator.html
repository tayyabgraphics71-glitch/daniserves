Frequently asked questions

What is an aggregate?

An aggregate is your weighted percentage. Each university gives a fixed share to Matric, FSc and its entry test, and adds them up to rank applicants.

Why are weights editable?

Universities change formulas by year, program and campus. If your prospectus shows different weights, type them in and the result updates.

Does a higher aggregate guarantee admission?

No. Admission depends on the closing merit of your chosen program and seats in that year. Compare your aggregate with last year's closing merit on the university's website.

Formulas are approximate and may change every admission cycle. Always confirm weights and closing merit in the official prospectus before applying. This tool is not affiliated with any university.
